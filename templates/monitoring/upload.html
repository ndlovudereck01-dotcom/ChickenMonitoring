What happens next

  1. ValidatedThe file is hashed and checked with OpenCV — a renamed image will be rejected.
  2. QueuedThe detection worker claims the job when a slot frees up; two jobs run at once by default.
  3. Detected & trackedYOLOv11 finds birds, DeepSORT keeps one identity per bird across frames.
  4. CountedUnique birds, peak occupancy, line crossings and zone activity are recorded.

The run page refreshes itself while processing and keeps the annotated video when it's done.

For long recordings, cut to the period you care about. Detection runs on CPU and processes roughly 2–6 frames per second depending on resolution.

Better results

  • Keep the camera still; panning confuses identity tracking.
  • Shoot from above at a moderate angle so birds don't overlap.
  • Avoid strong backlight and deep shadows.
  • Use a camera profile to reuse a tuned ROI and counting line.
